8.1 Either party may terminate this agreement for material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice from the party not in breach, specifying the nature of the breach and the remedy required.
8.2 Termination shall not affect any accrued rights or liabilities of either party as at the date of termination, nor any provision expressly stated to survive termination.
